# Sound credits

The robot's drive, firing and shot sounds and the HUB hits are recorded from our own team's robot and practice field. All other recordings are from [freesound.org](https://freesound.org) under the **Creative Commons 0**
license (public domain: no permission or credit needed; credited here anyway, with thanks).
They were trimmed, filtered, normalized and looped for the game.
