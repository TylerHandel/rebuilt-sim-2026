# Arena lighting

`crossfit_gym_1k.hdr`: "Crossfit Gym" from Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/crossfit_gym
(photography Dimitrios Savva, processing Jarod Guest), the 1k Radiance HDR as published
(https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/crossfit_gym_1k.hdr, md5
e0e07efadb05283e21f3a921433434d7). License: CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://polyhaven.com/license.

An indoor gym under a metal roof with rows of cool fluorescent tubes: js/graphics.js loads it
(RGBELoader, PMREM) as the image-based lighting and reflections from the Low preset up, with its
floor half darkened and grayed (a dark venue round the field); it isn't shown as the background.
